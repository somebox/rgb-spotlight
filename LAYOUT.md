# RGB Spotlight PCB layout

Status (2026-10-29): both boards routed. `pcbkit drc --save`: 0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 unconnected, 0 parity on both. `pcbkit check`: no floating copper, stubs or loops; the remaining thin-feed flags are deliberate (§Checks). Outline preset `disc50` on both. Next: settle the housing (outline preset), then PRODUCTION.md.

Board centre = origin, millimetres, top view, angles counter-clockwise from +x. Both boards are generated by scripts; the design reasons are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) (decisions D1–D13).

## Floor plan

### LED board (`boards/led`, aluminium, 1 layer)

`scripts/make_led_pcb.sh` generates the board, placed and routed.

- **Parameters,** at the top of `scripts/gen_led_pcb.py`:
  - `R_LED` 12: LEDs at R 90°, G 210°, B 330°, pads tangential;
  - `R_PAD` 6.2 / `PAD_SPREAD` 23°: six wire pads around the centre hole;
  - `D_WIRE_HOLE` 6.4;
  - islands r 8.6–22 mm, ±46°;
  - LED tracks 1.2 mm.
- **Copper islands:** each LED's centre (thermal) pad sits on its own island (`HEAT_R/G/B`). They never join (D8).
- **Wire hole:** a plain Edge.Cuts circle. A mounting-hole footprint would put a screw-head courtyard over the wire pads.
- **Order:** only F.Cu is used, so order it as a 1-layer aluminium board (PRODUCTION.md).

### Driver board (`boards/driver`, FR4, 2 layers)

`scripts/make_driver_pcb.sh --force --route` generates it: placement, then `scripts/route_driver.py`.

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| centre, front | J3 LED harness (PH 6 vertical); pin 1 at the top, towards the red cell; legend R+ … B− beside it |
| driver cells at 90° / 210° / 330° | identical (red rotated ±120°): Rs and L innermost (their LED-side pads face J3), then D, then the PT4115 with Cin |
| 150° gap | J1 IN on the **back** (pin row tangential at r = 19.5 mm); TVS D4 + C9, level shifter U7 + R7/R10/C3 on the front |
| 30° gap | J2 OUT on the **back**; 78L05 U5 + C4/C5 on the front |
| 270° gap | U6 74HC04 and U1 WS2811 side by side, pull-ups R4–R6 between them; C1/C2, R8/R9 |
| r = 11 mm, 30° / 150° / 270° | M3 standoff holes (`disc50`, `disc55`) |

**3D:** the SOT-89-5 model comes from LCSC (`lib/3dmodels/SOT-89-5_LCSC.step`, via easyeda2kicad C347356). It needs no rotation on the KiCad footprint: LCSC's footprint is KiCad's turned 180°, and its model carries that 180°.

## Stackup and rules

- **LED board:** aluminium, 1.6 mm, 1–2 W/mK; copper on F.Cu only. 0.3 mm clearance, 0.5 mm edge clearance.
- **Driver board:** FR4, 2 layers, 1.6 mm, GND pour on both layers. 0.2 mm clearance everywhere: the router routes to 0.2 mm, and 12 V needs no more.
- **Driver net classes:**
  - PWR 0.8 mm (+12V, GND), the taps' width. The 1.5 mm pass-through is drawn explicitly (D12).
  - LED 0.6 mm (CSN, LEDK, SW).
  - LOGIC_PWR 0.4 mm (+5V).
  - Default 0.25 mm.

## Scripted copper

`scripts/route_driver.py`, stage 0. The cells are scripted in red-cell coordinates and rotated ±120°. Each piece is checked against KiCad's exact pad shapes (not bounding boxes) before it is added:
- SW node U.SW → D.A → L.2 at 0.8 mm, front only. The hot loop is about 5 × 4 mm.
- 12 V cluster D.K → U.VIN, Cin.1 → U.VIN, and Rs.1 → via → D.K at 0.8 mm.
- Rs.2 → CSN Kelvin sense at 0.25 mm, round the outside of Cin.
- A DIM escape via beside each PT4115, and two GND vias at each tab.

The 12 V trunk is also scripted (stage 2). It is a 1.5 mm arc on B.Cu at r = 23.3 mm with radial stubs from J1.1 / J2.1, carrying the chain's pass-through current of up to 2.5–3 A.
- It runs from J1 (150°) over the top to the blue cell's tap (342°).
- The bottom of the ring fed nothing, so it was removed (2026-10-11, D12). `pcbkit check` flagged it as a loop.
- The router's clean-up (stage 6) removes the dead ends by itself on a fresh run.

## Routing

`scripts/route_driver.py` uses the maze router in `scripts/route_strip.py`, from the message-sign repo, extended for round outlines. Every route stays inside the 50 mm core. On 2026-10-11 there were 297 segments and 69 vias, after the ring was trimmed.

1. Cell links to J3 (LEDK_x, CSN_x).
2. +12V taps to the trunk: the cells, 78L05, TVS and C9, at 0.8 mm (0.4 mm where the full-search pass found them).
3. A GND via beside every front SMD GND pad (27). C4.2, at the edge, joins through the front pour.
4. Logic:
   - escape vias for the long cross-board pins (U7.4, U1.6, U1.1, R8.2, U6.2/4/6);
   - a scripted +5V spine for the pull-ups R4–R6;
   - then the maze: OUT/SET/DIN locally, DIM/DIN_5V/DOUT preferring the back, +5V last.
5. Full search (no corridor limit) for whatever the quick pass missed.
6. Clean-up: unused escape vias and dead-end copper go, then the zones are refilled.

**Known compromise:** the long logic runs (DIN_5V, DIM_R, +5V) cross the middle of the back. That splits the back GND plane into a few regions, all joined through the front pour and the stitching vias. That's fine for an 800 kHz data line and a 4 kHz PWM. A hand pass could move parts of those runs to the front.

**Regenerating:** the board comes from the scripts (`--force --route`, about 6 min). The 2026-10-11 ring edits were made in place, so a fresh run reproduces them only through the router's own clean-up. Once anything is edited by hand, stop regenerating and use *Update PCB from Schematic* (pcb-kit rule: never regenerate a routed board).

## Board outline presets

`scripts/mech.py` and `scripts/set_outline.py`. Parts, tracks and silk on both boards live inside the **50 mm core** (`mech.CORE_D`, D11). The board edge is a separate preset, so the housing decision doesn't touch the layout. Preview: `docs/previews/outline-presets.png`.

| Preset | Edge | Mounting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `disc50` (current) | 50 mm disc | inner M3: LED board r = 19 mm, driver r = 11 mm (30° / 150° / 270°) |
| `disc55` | 55 mm disc | inner M3 as above |
| `disc60` | 60 mm disc | M3 in the rim at r = 27.5 mm (30° / 150° / 270°); no inner holes |
| `tabs3` | 50 mm disc + three 9 mm tabs | M3 at r = 28.5 mm on the tabs (30° / 150° / 270°); no inner holes |

- **Choosing a preset:**
  - at generation: `MECH=<preset> scripts/make_led_pcb.sh` or `MECH=<preset> scripts/make_driver_pcb.sh --force --route`;
  - in place, even on a hand-edited board: `<KiCad python> scripts/set_outline.py <driver|led|all> <preset>`. Close the board in KiCad first.
- **What `set_outline.py` replaces:** only the edge, the H# holes, their keep-outs, the edge stitching and the GND pour extent.
  - The old outline is removed as a text edit, using `pcbkit.mech.top_level_blocks`.
  - The new one goes in through pcbnew, because `BOARD.Remove()` corrupts pcbnew's bindings (pcb-kit CONVENTIONS).
- **Holes outside the core** get a copper keep-out (r = 3.0 mm: an M3 pan head plus margin), so the screws touch no circuit copper.
- **Driver pour:** it grows with the outline, and is stitched front to back every 10° at r = 26 mm.
- **LED board:** the extra area is bare aluminium.
- **Tested** 2026-10-06: all four presets on both boards give DRC 0 / unconnected 0 / parity 0. Re-applying a preset is idempotent.

## Checks

```bash
pcbkit drc boards/driver/driver.kicad_pcb --save    # and boards/led/led.kicad_pcb
pcbkit check boards/driver/driver.kicad_pcb
pcbkit erc boards/driver/driver.kicad_sch && python3 scripts/netcheck.py
```

2026-10-11: DRC 0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 unconnected, 0 parity on both boards. `pcbkit check`: 0 floating, 0 stubs, 0 loops, 0 pours to one pad. The "power pads fed only by thin track" entries are all deliberate:

| Flag | Why it stays |
| --- | --- |
| `/CSN_R/G/B` (LED class 0.6 mm): U2.4, U3.4, U4.4 | the PT4115 CSN pin is fed by the 0.25 mm Kelvin sense line from Rs.2. It carries µA; the LED current goes Rs → J3 on its own 0.6 mm track |
| `+12V` (PWR 0.8 mm): the three cells (R/D/U/C), D4 | some taps were found by the full-search pass at 0.4 mm. A cell draws ≤ 0.15 A from 12 V (sim §2.7), and 0.4 mm of 1 oz copper carries that with no measurable rise. D4 (TVS) carries no steady current |
| LED board: LED1–LED3 pads (Default 1.2 mm) | each LED pad is 0.45 mm wide. The track leaves it at 0.45 mm for 2.1 mm, to clear the centre thermal pad by 0.3 mm, then widens to 1.2 mm |

`pcbkit cleanup --dry-run --dangling` (2026-10-29, pcb-kit 0.3.0): nothing to remove on either board. On the driver it would merge 7 collinear track pieces. That changes no copper, so it was left alone. KiCadRoutingTools' checkers are still to run before the order (PRODUCTION.md).

## Silkscreen

- **Driver front:** references placed by the kit's silk placer (`pcbkit.silk`); R5, J1, J2, D2 and C4 go to Fab (no free slot). The LED legend R+ … B− sits beside J3.
- **Driver back:** IN / OUT beside J1 / J2, the board name with its version ("RGB Spotlight driver v2.0", re-stamped by `pcbkit release`), the pinout ("1=12V 2=DATA 3=GND") and `JLCJLCJLCJLC` for the order number.
- **LED board:** colour letters by each LED, R+ … B− by the wire pads, "RGB LED v2.0" in the bare gap at 270°, at r = 12.2 mm (clear of H3 even at v2.0.10; `pcbkit release` re-stamps it).
