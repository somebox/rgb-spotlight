# RGB Spotlight v2.0 (driver + LED board): failure review

Status (2026-10-30): reviewed at `1610d73`. Covers both boards: 12 V input and chaining, 5 V logic, data path,
WS2811 and inverter, the three PT4115 channels, the LED board and harness, mounting, fab and assembly, firmware
duties. Not covered: EMI, the housing's thermal behaviour (nothing measured), KiCadRoutingTools' checkers (§Not covered).
Nothing in the design has been changed; the numbers are in `sim/results/worst_case.txt` (`sim/worst_case.py`).

Updated 2026-10-31 with the owner's answers (§Answers). R2 stays open, the §Power and data section is new, and R5's numbers are corrected. Accepted changes are planned in DESIGN §6 and not yet applied.

## Verdict

**Not ready to order yet.** The board and BOM changes are agreed and planned (DESIGN D17–D19) but not applied. Before ordering:
1. Apply them and re-run the checks.
2. Settle LED polarity at JLC (R1).

Neither the WS2811 (R2) nor the PT4115 stage has been proven on hardware, and v1 can't prove them. Order the smallest assembled run and treat it as the first test.

## Power and data

- **Power:** DESIGN §2.4 defines a feed (D14): 12 V, up to 5 lights, 26–28 W typical and 29–31 W worst case, 36 W supply for 4 lights plus controller, 60 W for 5. Numbers are in `sim/results/worst_case.txt`.
- **This board needs 12 V.** The PT4115 needs 6–30 V and stops below 5.1 V; the 78L05 has 1.7 V dropout, which leaves about 3.3 V from a 5 V input, under the WS2811's 3.5 V. A 5 V version would be a new driver at about 0.96 A per light, 3 lights per connector. A stepdown is fine for the controller, or from a higher supply down to 12 V.
- **Repeaters (D16):** pcb-kit's repeater is the SN74AHCT1G125, which each light already has as U7. Each WS2811 also regenerates the data, so nothing accumulates along a chain, and adding a gate on DOUT isn't needed.
- **Data over cable:** `sim/data_cable.py` drives a 250 ns T0H through R8 and a cable into R10 and U7, with assumed DO impedance and cable Z0. With R10 at 1 kΩ, every length to 8 m gives one clean edge each way, T0H about 277 ns at U7, and clamp current under 0.6 mA. With 100 Ω, the clamp takes 5–7 mA from 2 m up, so D18 also helps here. Not measured; bring-up step 6 scopes it.
- **Ground offset:** a downstream light's GND sits above its upstream neighbour's, by about 0.13 V per metre at 2.4 A in the first span. The data's low level therefore goes slightly negative at the next U7, not towards its threshold, and R10 limits that clamp current too (inferred).

## Verified OK

- **Pinouts against the datasheets, pad by pad on the board** (2026-10-30 pad dump):
  - **PT4115 SOT-89-5:** 1 SW, 2 GND (tab), 3 DIM, 4 CSN, 5 VIN. KiCad pad positions match UMW's drawing (1–3 on one side, 5/4 on the other, tab = GND).
  - **78L05G-AB3-R:** O-G-I (not the -AB3-C-R variant).
  - **SN74AHCT1G125 DCK:** 1 OE (GND), 2 A, 3 GND, 4 Y, 5 VCC.
  - **WS2811 SOP-8:** pins 1–8 as V2.2, with pin 7 NC on the DNP strap R9.
  - **74HC04:** pin 14 on +5V, unused inputs 9/11/13 to GND.
  - **Diodes:** B5819W and SMAJ15A cathodes on +12V.
  - **HD2525:** pad 1 K, pad 2 A, per its datasheet p.7.
  - **J3:** R+ R− G+ G− B+ B−.
- **PT4115 values:**
  - I_LED 422–476 mA at the corners, against 1 A LED abs max and the 0.082 Ω minimum Rs.
  - Inductor peak ≈ 0.58 A against Isat 1.1 A (×1.9; the datasheet asks 1.3–1.5×). Irms 1.3 A, DCR 376 mΩ (JLC data).
  - 68 µH is inside the datasheet's 47–68 µH band for 0.4–0.8 A.
  - Cin 10 µF 50 V 1206 X5R, above 4.7 µF at 12 V bias.
  - Schottky rated 40 V / 1 A against 12 V and ~0.35 A average (more than the datasheet's 30 % margin).
- **Voltages:**
  - The TVS clamps 24.4 V at 16 A, under the PT4115's 35 V abs max (VIN, SW) and the caps' 25/50 V.
  - The TVS's 15 V standoff is above a 12 V ± 10 % supply.
  - 78L05 dissipation 75–185 mW, against 350 mW.
- **Logic levels:**
  - AHCT VIH 2.0 V takes a 3.3 V controller.
  - The AHCT's 5 V output meets the WS2811's VIH of 0.55 × VDD.
  - DIM high 4.8–5.2 V: under the 6 V abs max. The PWM formula assumes ≤ 5 V; at 5.2 V nothing changes but the margin.
  - The WS2811 output pulls the 4.7 kΩ pull-up fully low (16 mA sink against 1.06 mA).
- **Unpowered and partial states:**
  - AHCT inputs have no clamp to VCC, so an upstream light can't back-power an unpowered one through the data line (TI datasheet; pcb-kit PARTS.md).
  - The WS2811's DIN/DO survive a momentary 24 V (V2.2 features).
  - With no controller, R7 holds DIN low.
  - v1 measured the WS2811 outputs off at power-up (DIM_x_RAW at 5 V), so DIM should be low and the light dark until data arrives. Not settled, given v1's faults: check at bring-up step 3.
  - If data stops, the WS2811 holds its last frame (V2.2). Full white held indefinitely is inside the thermal design (§2.6).
- **Harness faults (from the topology, inferred):**
  - J3 unplugged under power: no current path, so the switch just stays on. Plugging back in ramps through L.
  - LED shorted: the channel still regulates through L.
- **Copper:**
  - The 12 V trunk is 1.5 mm of 1 oz, ≈ 3.2 A at a 10 °C rise (IPC-2221), with full-width stubs to J1.1/J2.1.
  - The GND pass-through is the pours on both layers. The back pour is split down the middle by the logic runs, but its halves join round the bottom and through the front pour (netview).
  - The mounting holes are unplated (NPTH), so no copper connects to the screws.
- **Checks on 2026-10-29:** ERC 0, DRC 0/0 with parity 0 on both boards, `netcheck.py` 0 failures, `pcbkit check` clean (thin feeds explained in LAYOUT.md), `pcbkit release --dry-run` passes.

## Test points and rework provisions

The driver has no test points, and its vias are tented. Most nets can still be probed at a part's pin:
- **SW:** the inductor or diode pads.
- **CSN and current:** across Rs (1206): 100 mV = 455 mA.
- **DIN_5V and the WS2811 outputs:** the SOIC pins of U1.
- **DIM:** the 74HC04 pins.
- **+5V:** at C1 to C4.

## Bench list

**Before ordering:** diode-test a v1 LED for its polarity (R1). It tests the part, so v1's faults don't matter. Everything else moves to v2 bring-up, because v1 results can't be relied on.

**v2 bring-up, per board:**
1. **Inspect:** LED polarity against the "+" marks (R1); the pin-1 dots of U1–U7 (R11); D1–D4 bands.
2. **Unpowered:** resistance from +12V to GND (≫ 1 kΩ once C6–C9 have charged from the meter) and from +5V to GND. From the housing / mounting screws to GND and +5V: open (R8). From each harness wire to the LED board's aluminium: open (R9).
3. **First power:** 12 V from a supply limited to 0.15 A, with no data and J3 plugged in. Expect:
   - +5V = 4.8–5.2 V at U1.8;
   - the light dark;
   - less than 30 mA drawn.

   If all three colours light at full, check +5V (R12).
4. **One colour at a time:** raise the limit to 0.6 A and send one colour at 255, at 800 kHz with a ≥ 500 µs gap. Expect about 100 mV across that colour's Rs and about 0.15 A drawn. Scope DIM_x at a PWM edge for chatter (R4).
5. **Full white, closed housing, 30 min:**
   - 0.42 A per light;
   - D1's case temperature (R10);
   - J2's temperature on the first light of a chain;
   - listen for singing (R13).
6. **Chain:** two lights with a pin-1-to-pin-1 XH cable (tested with a meter first), then 5 on one feed at full white. Scope U7 pin 2 on the longest cable.
7. **Brightness against level (0–32):** for a gamma table (R13).

## Firmware duties

Checked against V2.2 on 2026-10-31, using FastLED master (`fl/chipsets/led_timing.h`) and ESPHome dev (`esp32_rmt_led_strip/light.py`). Other versions may differ.

| Driver | T0H / T0L | T1H / T1L | Reset | Against V2.2 (T0H 220–380; others 580–1000 ns; reset ≥ 280 µs) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FastLED `WS2811` | 250 / 1000 ns | 600 / 650 ns | 280 µs | inside, T0L and reset exactly at the limit |
| FastLED `WS2811_400` | 500 / 2000 ns | 1200 / 1300 ns | 280 µs | outside: don't use |
| ESPHome `chipset: WS2811` | 300 / 1090 ns | 1090 / 320 ns | 300 µs | **outside:** T0L and T1H long, T1L short |
| ESPHome custom timing (below) | 300 / 950 ns | 650 / 600 ns | 300 µs | inside |

- **FastLED:** use `WS2811`, and leave a gap (`delayMicroseconds(500)`) if frames are sent back to back. Nothing was measured on hardware.
- **Home Assistant, assuming ESPHome on an ESP32:** leave `chipset` out of `esp32_rmt_led_strip` and set the timing. WLED was not checked.

  ```yaml
  bit0_high: 300ns
  bit0_low: 950ns
  bit1_high: 650ns
  bit1_low: 600ns
  reset_low: 300us
  ```
- **Colour order:** R, G, B, MSB first. No inversion in software: the 74HC04 already makes 255 = full.
- **Gamma correction:** keep levels below ~8 out of fades (R13).
- **Power budget:** up to 5.51 W per light at full white in the worst case, 5 lights per feed (DESIGN §2.4).
